Projects

In addition to (and intertwining with) my teaching, research, and digital work, I am deeply engaged with campus communities through contributing to career education and archival projects.

Career Education

My research in career education synergizes with my ongoing work in advising, administration, and cross-campus committees.

As a career advisor at UMass Amherst's Humanities & Fine Arts Career Services, I

  • Advised about 80 students per semester one-on-one on resumes, cover letters, portfolios, the job search process, and career questions.
  • Presented on career topics (such as determining paths of interest, networking, and interviewing) during class visits.
  • Designed career resources for students such as application guides and resources by identity and areas of interest.

I also supported career education across campus, including

  • Collaborating with advisors and instructors to revise language in their websites, resources, and syllabi to be more inclusive.
  • Co-leading weekly "Beyond Professionalism" Working Group meetings where particpants discuss strategies for talking to students about professionalism in more just ways.
  • Presenting on-campus workshops on topics of career education and equity.
  • Participating in Careers Marketing Working Group meetings and contributing ideas of inclusivity and accessibility.

Archives

Interning and researching in archives has allowed me to explore and recover histories as well as find practical uses for archival records.

A recent project involved the UMass Amherst Writing Program Archives. I first processed the archival materials through arrangement of records, labeling series and files, and creating a finding aid. I then increased access to these archives even more by digitizing records for the Writing Program's Digital Archives.
