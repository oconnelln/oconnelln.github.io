Dissertation

"Envisioning and Redesigning Career Education Rhetoric: A Human-Centered Approach"

Committee: David Fleming (Chair), Janine Solberg, and Johanna Yunker

Abstract: Through a case study of the University of Massachusetts Amherst involving feminist rhetorical analysis on university career education, focus groups with students and educators, and student surveys, I devise recommendations for human-centered career education that embraces humanities, minimizes oppression, emphasizes empowerment, and is informed by students and educators.

I believe research should lead to action! Find out more about my work with career education in my Three Minute Thesis presentation and in the "career education" section of my website.
